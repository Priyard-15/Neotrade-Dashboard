3. **What is India's current GDP growth trend?**
4. **Name a recent government initiative.**
5. **What are the challenges facing India today?**
6. **Who is the current President of India?** (Droupadi Murmu)
7. **What is BRICS?**
8. **What is the significance of G20?**
9. **What recent technology innovation interests you?**
10. **How is AI impacting jobs?**

## Best Interview Preparation Search

Every day, search:

* "Top 10 current affairs today"
* "Current affairs for interviews"
* "Latest AI news"
* "Business news today India"
* "One important international news today"

Then practice answering:

> "A recent news item that interested me was ________. It is important because ________. This could impact ________."

This structure helps you sound confident in interviews and public discussions.
